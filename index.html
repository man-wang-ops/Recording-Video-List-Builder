2. PPD links

Add a PPD link for every week of your course. The week name becomes a clickable link in the PDF, and you can’t save until every week in your outline has one. You can also enter the estimated total minutes for each week (for example, 60). It appears in small gray text under the week name in the PDF.

3. Video types and colors

The first type is the default for videos with no tag and has no color. For a tag like [Lecture + iPad], the first matching type below the default wins, so it is colored as iPad. Keywords are matched against the text inside the brackets.

4. Paste your outline

One video per line. Put the type in [brackets] and the length as “5 min”. Start each week with “Week 1”. A “Lesson 1:” line groups the sections under it; a lesson with no sections under it is treated as one video.
